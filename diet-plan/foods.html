01 / BUILD FROM THESE
PROTEIN
One at every meal
  • Chicken thigh or breast
  • Beef mince, 5% fat
  • Lamb chops
  • Eggs, any way
  • Tinned tuna, salmon, mackerel
  • Prawns
  • Greek yoghurt, 0% or 5%
  • Cottage cheese
  • Halloumi or feta
  • Lentils and chickpeas
FIBRE + VEG
Fill half the plate
  • Spinach, kale, spring greens
  • Broccoli and cauliflower
  • Courgette, aubergine
  • Peppers, onions, garlic
  • Cucumber, tomato, salad
  • Mushrooms
  • Green beans
  • Avocado
  • Cabbage or salad slaw
  • Okra
SMART CARBS
A cupped handful, earlier in the day
  • Oats
  • Sweet potato
  • New or baby potatoes
  • Basmati or brown rice
  • Wholemeal wrap or pitta
  • Quinoa or bulgur
  • Beans, any kind
DRINKS
Free all day
  • Water, aim 2 litres
  • Black coffee
  • Tea, no sugar
  • Sparkling water
  • Diet drinks, sparingly
FRUIT
Some days, not every day
  • Berries
  • Apple or pear
  • Orange or satsuma
  • Kiwi
  • Banana, after a walk
FLAVOUR, FREE
Use as much as you like
  • Garlic, ginger, chilli
  • Lemon and lime
  • Herbs and spices
  • Mustard, vinegar
  • Hot sauce, no sugar
02 / MEALS THAT TAKE TEN MINUTES
BREAKFAST

Start with protein

  1. Three eggs scrambled, spinach, one slice wholemeal
  2. Greek yoghurt, berries, spoon of nuts
  3. Oats made with milk, protein stirred in
  4. Omelette with feta, tomato and peppers
LUNCH

Build it cold if you're out

  1. Chicken, salad and slaw in a wholemeal wrap
  2. Tuna, sweetcorn, cucumber, big salad
  3. Leftover dinner in a tub — cook one extra portion
  4. Chickpeas, feta, tomato, olive oil, lemon
DINNER

Protein, veg, small carb

  1. Grilled chicken thighs, roast veg, few potatoes
  2. Beef mince, onions, peppers, half a portion of rice
  3. Salmon, green beans, sweet potato
  4. Lamb chops, salad, grilled aubergine
IF YOU'RE HUNGRY

Snacks that hold

  1. Boiled eggs
  2. Greek yoghurt
  3. Small handful of nuts
  4. Cucumber and hummus
03 / STRAIGHT SWAPS
SAME MEAL, BETTER VERSION
White rice→Half portion, extra veg
Bread with dinner→Extra veg or salad
Crisps→Nuts, a small handful
Fizzy drink→Sparkling water with lemon
Chocolate bar→Greek yoghurt with berries
Late night cereal→Nothing — kitchen's shut
Fried chicken→Grilled, skin off, extra salad
Sugar in tea→One less each week
04 / PORTIONS WITHOUT SCALES
Palm

Protein. One palm per meal, two if you trained.

Two fists

Veg and salad. Hard to overdo.

Cupped hand

Rice, potato, pasta, oats.

Thumb

Oil, butter, nut butter, cheese.
