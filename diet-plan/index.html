03 / RECOMMENDED FOODS

Shop from this list and the diary writes itself.

PROTEIN
Build every meal around one
  • Chicken thigh or breast
  • Beef mince, 5% fat
  • Lamb chops
  • Eggs, any way
  • Tinned tuna, salmon, mackerel
  • Prawns
  • Greek yoghurt, 0% or 5%
  • Cottage cheese
  • Halloumi or feta
  • Lentils and chickpeas
FIBRE + VEG
Fill half the plate
  • Spinach, kale, spring greens
  • Broccoli and cauliflower
  • Courgette, aubergine
  • Peppers, onions, garlic
  • Cucumber, tomato, salad
  • Mushrooms
  • Green beans
  • Avocado
  • Cabbage or salad slaw
  • Okra
FRUIT
Some days, not every day
  • Berries
  • Apple or pear
  • Orange or satsuma
  • Kiwi
  • Banana, after a walk
DRINKS
Free all day
  • Water, aim 2 litres
  • Black coffee
  • Tea, no sugar
  • Sparkling water
  • Diet drinks, sparingly
STRAIGHT SWAPS
White rice→Cauliflower rice or half portion
Bread with dinner→Extra veg or salad
Crisps→Nuts, a small handful
Fizzy drink→Sparkling water with lemon
Chocolate bar→Greek yoghurt with berries
Late night cereal→Nothing — kitchen's shut
